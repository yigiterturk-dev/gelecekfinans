# GelecekFinans Core Brief

Version: `2026-08-06.v1`

## Identity and current state

GelecekFinans is a Turkish finance publication and consumer-finance toolset running on Next.js, Prisma/Neon and Vercel. The public design, logo, typography and existing market/tool surfaces are supplied and remain locked for this revision.

## Business

- Primary model: useful recurring finance traffic monetized with AdSense.
- Secondary future models: newsletter, product referrals and finance-related partnerships.
- Current bottleneck: the automated newsroom produced generic articles, then publication stopped; the site no longer feels current.

## Purpose and success

Turn the existing site into a trustworthy daily finance utility instead of an automated article farm.

Success signals:

- reliable and source-backed editorial output;
- rejected low-confidence drafts never reach production;
- useful recurring surfaces such as market data, calculators and calendar-like updates;
- clear Search Console, analytics, consent and AdSense readiness;
- rising returning-user and tool-use rates rather than raw article count.

## Users

Turkish readers checking exchange rates, gold, crypto, interest and everyday financial calculations, primarily on mobile. They need speed, plain language, source clarity and explicit data timestamps.

## First release scope

- Audit and harden the autonomous publishing pipeline.
- Preserve the existing public visual identity.
- Close trust, SEO, consent and AdSense-readiness gaps that can be handled safely in code.
- Improve the site's recurring-use loop without inventing unsupported market data.
- Validate locally before any production decision.

## Later scope

- Watchlists and price alerts.
- Economic calendar.
- Personalized newsletter and saved preferences.
- Referral and partner integrations.

## Exclusions

- No cross-project visual or content inheritance from HirdavatPro or EmlakAklı.
- No production deployment without current explicit user approval.
- No fabricated news, financial claims, quotations or market data.
